# Bitburner Augmentation Progression Route

A step-by-step roadmap to take you from a fresh game all the way to unlocking the best end-game corporate Augmentations. This route optimizes hacking multipliers and reputation speeds to bypass long grinds.

---

## 🗓️ Phase 1: The Foundation (Early Game)

**Goal:** Setup basic multipliers, unlock automatic network pathfinding, and boost hacking stats.

### Step 1: Sector-12 (City Faction)

- **Conditions to Join:** Travel to Sector-12 (Starting location) and have $15,000.
- **What to buy:**
  - `CashRoot Starter Kit` (Gives early money/ram).
  - `Neuralstimulator` (+10% Hacking speed/chance/exp).
- **When to Install/Reset:** Install as soon as you can afford both. This is your first soft reset.

### Step 2: CyberSec

- **Conditions to Join:** Find the `CSEC` server (usually around Hacking level 50+), run `NUKE.exe`, and install a `backdoor`.
- **What to buy:**
  - `Cranium Mappers` (+20% Hacking speed).
  - `BitWire` (+5% Hacking level).
- **When to Install/Reset:** Buy these immediately after your first reset, grind CyberSec rep, buy them, and reset again.

---

## 📈 Phase 2: The Reputation & Charisma Boom (Mid Game)

**Goal:** Drastically increase your reputation gain rate so corporate grinding doesn't take days.

### Step 3: Tian Di Hui

- **Conditions to Join:** Have $1,000,000 and 50 Hacking level. Travel to **Chongqing** or **New Tokyo** to trigger the invite.
- **What to buy:**
  - `Social Negotiation Assistant (S.N.A)` [30m] (+15% Faction&Company Rep, +15% Cha EXP).
  - `Speech Processor Implant` [50m] (+20% Charisma).

### Step 4: Bachman & Associates (First Corp Target)

- **Conditions to Join:** Travel to **Aevum**. Reach **225 Hacking/Charisma**, apply for a job, and grind to **400,000 Company Reputation** (or use Infiltration).
- **What to buy:**
  - `SmartJaw` [2.75b] (+50% Charisma skill and EXP, +25% Faction&Company Rep gain).
  - `ADR-V2 Pheromone Gene` [550m] (+20% Company Rep gain, +10% Charisma).
- **When to Install/Reset:** **Crucial Step.** Grind Bachman until you have both of these. Once installed, your Charisma and future Faction reputation speeds are permanently supercharged.

---

## 🚀 Phase 3: The Hacking Power Push (Late-Mid Game)

**Goal:** Scale your Hacking Level toward that massive 1476 requirement.

### Step 5: NiteSec & The Black Hand

- **Conditions to Join NiteSec:** Backdoor the `avmnite-02h` server (requires ~Hacking 200).
- **Conditions to Join Black Hand:** Backdoor the `I.I.I.I` server (requires ~Hacking 350).
- **What to buy:**
  - `Artificial Synaptic Potentiation` (+15% Hacking speed/exp).
  - `The Black Hand` (+10% Hacking level, +15% Hacking speed/money/chance).
- **When to Install/Reset:** Accumulate these massive hacking stat augmentations together. Install them to jump your Hacking level straight into the high hundreds.

### Step 6: Volhaven & OmniTek Incorporated (Optional Boost)

- **Conditions to Join OmniTek:** Travel to **Volhaven**, hit 225 Hacking, work the job to **200,000 Reputation**.
- **What to buy:**
  - `OmniTek InfoLoad` (+25% Hacking server yield and EXP).
- **When to Install/Reset:** Only grab this if your Hacking level is slowing down before hitting the Fulcrum requirements.

---

## 👑 Phase 4: The End Game Targets

**Goal:** Break the economy and unlock the final server backdoor.

### Step 7: Fulcrum Secret Technologies (Second Corp Target)

- **Conditions to Join:**
  1.  Travel to **Aevum**, get a job at Fulcrum Technologies (Hacking 250), and grind to **250,000 Corporate Reputation**.
  2.  Reach **1476 Hacking Level** and execute `backdoor` on the `fulcrumassets` server.
- **What to buy:**
  - `The Red Pill` (Cost: 0 Rep. Absolutely essential. Allows you to backdoor the final `w0r1d_d43m0n` server to beat the BitNode).
- **When to Install/Reset:** Do **not** reset immediately after buying this unless you have other augmentations you want to lock in. It stays active until you choose to use it.

### Step 8: ECorp (Final Target)

- **Conditions to Join:** Travel to **Aevum**, get a job at ECorp (Hacking 250), and grind to **200,000 Corporate Reputation**.
- **What to buy:**
  - `ECorp HVMind Implant` (Requires massive reputation, usually around 1.5M faction rep total).
- **When to Install/Reset:** This is your victory lap augmentation. Buy it, install it, and use its +200% Grow power to generate trillions of dollars while you prepare to hack the final server.

---

## 💡 Summary Order of Operations

1. **Sector-12** (Early Cash/RAM) ➔ **Reset 1**
2. **CyberSec** (Early Hack Multipliers) ➔ **Reset 2**
3. **Tian Di Hui** + **Bachman & Associates** (Reputation Multipliers) ➔ **Reset 3**
4. **NiteSec** + **The Black Hand** (Massive Hacking Level Push) ➔ **Reset 4**
5. **Fulcrum** (Get "The Red Pill") + **ECorp** (Get "HVMind Implant") ➔ **Final Run to Victory**
